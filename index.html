Why a regulated RCIC? Only licensed RCICs are legally authorized to provide immigration advice for a fee in Canada. Unregulated consultants — however cheap — put your entire immigration future at risk. I am bound by the CICC Code of Professional Ethics, which means you are protected.

What I do

Refused Case Reapplication
My core focus. A previous refusal is not the end of the road — whatever category you applied under. I review your refusal letter, order fresh GCMS notes where needed, and build a completely new strategy that directly addresses the officer's concerns. Read more about refused cases →
Refusal Letter Analysis & GCMS Notes
Understand exactly why your application was refused. I decode the officer's notes and give you an honest read on whether — and how — your case can be strengthened before you spend money reapplying. Read more about GCMS notes →
Temporary Resident Applications
Visitor visas and Super Visas, study permits and PGWP, open and employer-specific work permits, and Temporary Resident Permits — prepared, reviewed, and submitted through the IRCC portal. Read more about the Super Visa →
Permanent Residence & Express Entry
Express Entry profiles across Federal Skilled Worker, Federal Skilled Trades, and Canadian Experience Class, plus Provincial Nominee Program streams — including honest eligibility assessment before you commit.
Family & Spousal Sponsorship
Sponsorship applications for spouses, partners, children, and parents — with careful attention to the relationship evidence and financial requirements that these applications turn on.
Fully Remote, Worldwide
Consultations via Microsoft Teams, documents by email, agreements signed digitally, payments by e-Transfer or credit card. The same high standard of care wherever you are in the world.
