Canada visitor visa for UAE residents.

I lived in Dubai for 11 years before moving to Canada, so I know how a UAE residence visa, an employer letter and a Dubai bank account look from both sides of the application. This is the guide I wish my friends there had.

By Kevin Thibbles, RCIC Published October 30, 2026 Last reviewed October 30, 2026 11 min read
Send me an enquiry Message me on WhatsApp

First, check what your passport needs

Whether you need a visitor visa depends on your passport, not on where you live. Your UAE residence visa doesn't change it. Here is how it works for the nationalities I met most often in the UAE:

Your passportWhat you need to fly to Canada
United Arab Emirates, QatarAn eTA (electronic travel authorization), not a visitor visa
PhilippinesA visitor visa, or an eTA if you've held a Canadian visa in the last 10 years or hold a valid U.S. non-immigrant visa, and you're flying
India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, NepalA visitor visa
Egypt, Jordan, LebanonA visitor visa
Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, BahrainA visitor visa

Based on IRCC's entry requirements by country, checked October 30, 2026. Always confirm with IRCC's own tool before you apply.

If you only need an eTA, the process is short and online. The rest of this guide is for everyone who needs a visitor visa, which is most UAE residents.

What the officer is deciding

IRCC lists the basic requirements: a valid passport, good health, no criminal or immigration-related convictions, enough money for your stay, ties that will take you home, and convincing the officer you'll leave Canada at the end of your visit. You may also need a medical exam or a letter of invitation from someone living in Canada.

For UAE residents, most visitor visa refusals come back to the same question: will this person leave Canada on time? The officer can't ask you in person, so your documents have to answer it.

How ties work when you live in the UAE

Your ties are split between two places, and both count.

  • Your life in the UAE. A steady job, a residence visa that stays valid well beyond your trip, a home lease, children in school, years of continuous residence.
  • Your home country. Property, family, investments, or a clear plan to return there eventually.
  • Your money. Savings that match your income and your plans, rather than a sudden large deposit with no explanation.
  • Your travel history. Previous trips to Canada, the U.S., the U.K., Europe or elsewhere where you returned on time.

I explain how officers read a Gulf residence permit in more detail in How your UAE residence visa affects the officer's decision.

Documents that carry weight for UAE residents

  • Your passport and your UAE residence visa and Emirates ID, valid for the whole trip and ideally well beyond it.
  • An employer letter on company letterhead: your role, salary, start date, approved leave dates, and that your job continues when you return. A salary certificate helps.
  • Bank statements from your UAE account showing salary credits and savings over several months.
  • Your tenancy contract (in Dubai, the Ejari registration), or proof of the home you own.
  • Evidence of ties at home: property papers, family details, investments.
  • A clear travel plan, and an invitation letter if you're staying with family or friends in Canada.
  • Copies of previous visas and entry stamps.
A common mistake

Applying with a residence visa that expires during or soon after the trip. If you're renewing, consider waiting for the new visa before you apply, or explain the renewal clearly with evidence.

Fees, biometrics and where to apply

  • You apply online through your IRCC account.
  • The visitor visa fee starts at CAD $100. Most applicants also give biometrics (fingerprints and a photo) for CAD $85.
  • Biometrics are taken at a Canada Visa Application Centre run by VFS Global. Use IRCC's visa application centre finder for the current UAE locations.

How long it takes from the UAE

IRCC's current estimate for visitor visas applied for from the UAE is 55 days, updated October 29, 2026. That doesn't include the time to give biometrics, so plan for longer.

Mistakes I see most often

  • Leaving the purpose of the trip vague. "Tourism" alone tells the officer very little.
  • Large, unexplained deposits shortly before applying.
  • Leaving out a previous refusal from any country. Always disclose it. Hiding it can lead to a finding of misrepresentation, which is far more serious than a refusal.
  • Reapplying quickly with the same documents after a refusal. See Refused while living in the UAE.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for a Canadian visitor visa from the UAE if I'm not an Emirati citizen?

Yes. Many people apply from the UAE on a residence visa. The officer will look at your status and life in the UAE as well as your ties to your home country, so include your residence visa, Emirates ID and evidence of your job and home there.

Do Emirati citizens need a visa for Canada?

No. UAE citizens need an electronic travel authorization (eTA) to fly to Canada, not a visitor visa, according to IRCC's entry requirements by country.

How much does a Canadian visitor visa cost from the UAE?

The visitor visa fee starts at CAD $100, and most applicants also pay CAD $85 for biometrics. The visa application centre may charge its own service fees.

How long does a Canadian visitor visa take from the UAE?

IRCC publishes a processing time for applications from the UAE that changes over time. My wait-times tracker shows the latest figure, and this page updates automatically. Biometrics time is extra.

I'm Filipino and live in Dubai. Can I get an eTA instead?

Possibly. Filipino citizens can apply for an eTA instead of a visa when flying to Canada if they've held a Canadian visa in the last 10 years or currently hold a valid U.S. non-immigrant visa. Otherwise a visitor visa is needed.
